## Q1. What is the exact sequence of screens from a cold first launch (no account) to the first counted bottle?

### Takeaway
A brand-new user lands on the **sign-IN screen** (headed "Welcome back"), not on sign-up. Email path: Start Free Trial → 4-field form plus a required terms checkbox → "What's your bar called?" (skippable) → camera **start screen** (3 tips + camera-access note) → Start Scanning → iOS camera prompt → live camera → shutter → number pad opens at once → type a count → Add. The Apple path replaces the first two steps with one "Continue with Apple" tap on the login screen plus Apple's own sheet. Nothing asks for a location, staff name, distributor, par or price before the first scan; a location called "My Bar" is created silently. Camera is the only permission requested.

## Q2. After the first count: what do Review & Par and Order Summary require before any value, and how many steps to a first sent order?

### Takeaway
The first order is where setup is concentrated. Before an order can even be generated, **every identified bottle needs a par** (hard block, tap-only stepper starting at 1). Only bottles below 0.7 × par become order lines. **Every order line needs a distributor** (name + order email, created inline from Review; Order Summary can only pick existing ones). If the bar name was skipped, a modal asks for it at send. Account numbers and prices are optional; case vs. bottle is decided automatically. For N bottles from one new distributor, a first order costs roughly N × (par taps) + 2 × (N − 1) + 7 taps plus ~30–45 typed characters.

**Step/tap estimate (first order, one new distributor, N identified bottles; assumptions stated)**
- Done (1) → par per bottle (≥1 tap; par P from the default 1 costs P − 1 taps on +) → first bottle "Assign →" (1) → "Add a Distributor →" (1) → type name → tap email field (1) → type email → "Save & Assign" (1) → each other bottle "Assign →" + pick (2 each) → "Generate Order Summary" (1) → "Confirm & Email Distributors" (1) [+ bar-name modal: type + "Save & Send" (1) if skipped]. Formula ≈ N·(P−1) + 2·(N−1) + 7 taps (min 1 par tap per bottle), plus ~30–45 characters of distributor name/email. Example: 20 bottles at par 6 → 20·5 + 38 + 7 = 145 taps. Derived from the controls cited above ([src/screens/ReviewGrid.tsx:379-485](/home/user/86d-mobile/src/screens/ReviewGrid.tsx), [src/screens/ReviewGrid.tsx:722-746](/home/user/86d-mobile/src/screens/ReviewGrid.tsx), [src/screens/OrderSummary.tsx:882-899](/home/user/86d-mobile/src/screens/OrderSummary.tsx)).

### Inferences
- A user who sets pars equal to what they have (a natural first guess) gets "All Stocked!" — nothing to send — because lines need stock below 70% of par; the footer's "AI reorders a bottle below 0.7 of its par" is the only explanation, and it calls a fixed arithmetic rule "AI".
- Arriving at Order Summary with no distributor is a dead end: the sheet says "No distributors added yet." with no way to add one there; the user must find Review & Par or Settings through the menu.
- The par step is the most tap-expensive part of the whole first run (no typing in Review, +1 per tap from 1), and it is mandatory for every bottle before any order exists.
- On bad wifi, a still-unidentified row with stock 0 can surface as an "Unknown bottle"/"Identifying…" order line that needs a distributor and blocks the send — an edge case the par gate does not catch.
- Because the default location is "My Bar" and no step asks to rename it, a new bar's first distributor email likely reads "… at The Corner Tavern (My Bar)."

### Gaps
- Real numbers of bottles per first count, typical pars, and how often first orders hit "All Stocked!" are not visible from code (would need `par_levels`/`orders` data; production was not queried).
- Whether the keyboard hides "Save & Assign" or the email field in the distributor sheet was not verified on a device.

## Q7. What first-run measurement exists, and which steps are blind?

### Takeaway
Client analytics cover only five **pre-account** events, and only for the **email** path: an Apple sign-up fires no event, so the app funnel's "created_account" excludes Apple accounts.
